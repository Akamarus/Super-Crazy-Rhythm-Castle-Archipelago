# v0.29.1 — Player movement repair

**October 12, 2026. Client 0.76.1 / APWorld 0.29.0 (unchanged).**

## Fixed

- Fixed the captured player-movement stalls where animation stayed smooth but movement repeatedly slowed or stopped, including after songs and area transitions. Very short input-movement updates now accumulate their elapsed time before being applied, avoiding the observed acceleration resets.
- Kept native rendering and frame-rate preferences; this repair does not force fullscreen or a 50 FPS cap.

## Diagnostics and documentation

- Expanded opt-in F8 movement captures with bounded player-update timing records, movement speed values and repair counters. Detailed recording is active only during a capture.
- Reorganized the README and current documentation, removed contradictory setup/version guidance, and archived superseded plans and test checkpoints with an index.
- Updated the current issue list and recorded the newly completed gameplay acceptance tests.

## Updating

Close the game and replace the client files with **RhythmCastleAP-v0.76.1.zip**, retaining the included licenses. Keep your configuration, current seed and associated save. The APWorld remains **0.29.0**; its logic, IDs, options, item placements, bundle mappings and Star gates are unchanged. An APWorld download is included for convenience, not as a required upgrade from 0.29.0.

## Validation

The movement implementation was accepted in gameplay before its version-only release bump: initial movement, after a Music Lab song, room changes, restart, Hypno Pan NPC movement, stopping, reversing, gentle analogue input, solid-wall collision and pause/resume. Diagnostic samples confirmed the repair active.

Automated coverage includes player timing boundaries, one normal and three faulty recorded timing streams, the timing recorder, movement capture, Montage timing, notification bursts and cassette bundles. All 44 client regression projects passed for publication. The Release build, repository validator, 10 packaging tests and final archive checks passed. NuGet vulnerability metadata could not be fetched; this is not a completed dependency-security audit. Managed tests do not prove every native configuration.

Additional tests confirmed Star-blocked entrance retry without area reload; immediate item receipt/deposit and persistence; original Plant Pipes/Hip Glasses pickups; Meoo/Plunger/Star Eater checks; Superstar insertion and medal preview; and Quicksand/Bloody Tears chests without duplicate vanilla rewards. These validate existing behavior rather than introduce new features in this patch.

## Known limits

Intermittent note-affecting performance stalls remain under investigation; the movement repair is not a claim that all frame hitches are fixed. Extended-session and multiplayer movement coverage remains incomplete. Triple cassette bundles and large notification batches have automated coverage but less native acceptance than the tested paths. Native route/modeling uncertainties remain listed in [known issues](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md).
