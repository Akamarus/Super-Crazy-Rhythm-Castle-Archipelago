# v0.28.1 — First Verified Victory

**Client v0.75.13 / APWorld v0.28.0 · September 21, 2026**

A fresh-save Hard playthrough with a 40 AP Star goal has now completed without
server assistance. King Ferdinand I was cleared with 41 AP Stars; the client saved
the qualifying result and the server recorded CLIENT_GOAL (30). This verifies one
complete route, not every seed, difficulty or multiplayer configuration.

## Changes since the September 18 v0.28.0 hotfix

- Fixed Victory rejecting the native completion event when its optional variant is absent. The exact level/variant is already verified against the saved score. Conflicting events still fail validation.
- Retained qualifying persisted clears while native-save readiness is temporarily unavailable, with same-save checks, durable journaling and reconnect delivery.
- Classified the Tower of Fear exterior entrance as Tower of Fear. Without access, arrivals from Royal Corridor now redirect to Music Lab, preventing the no-exit trap. Automated regression passed; live testing was explicitly deferred.
- Opened Royal Corridor traversal for its AP Access item independently of the Star Eater feeding threshold. Feeding remains a separate check and does not consume stars.
- Added clearer in-game level-entry messages showing required and missing AP Stars.
- Improved queued item notifications, wrapping and scrolling in F6 history, and long-message handling so reward batches remain accessible. Sent-item replay deduplication now covers the whole slot.
- Suppressed native cassette rewards and popups from the five audited Music Lab chest sources (Quicksand, Flamenco, Ten-Four Good Buddy, Zen and Wiggle), preserving randomized checks and native insertion. Previously leaked ownership is not erased automatically.
- Fixed cassette source checks being skipped when the corresponding AP cassette was received early.
- Tightened AP Star history validation, new-save identity checks and stale expanded-check snapshot handling.
- Reduced unnecessary character-event reflection/allocations and removed obsolete recursive save diagnostics and automatic detail dumps. Detailed diagnostics are opt-in.
- Added an optional bounded F8 movement capture for investigating movement faults. This is diagnostic support, not a movement fix.

## Updating

Close the game and replace the three plugin DLLs using **RhythmCastleAP-v0.75.13.zip**.
Existing v0.28 seeds and saves remain compatible. The included **scrc.apworld** keeps
the v0.28.0 / schema-19 contract; no new IDs, progression placements or goal rules
are introduced by this maintenance release. Connect to your seed before loading.
Existing boss clears whose completion was discarded are not reconstructed from
save flags; a qualifying clear on the repaired client reports Victory normally.

## Known issues and testing limits

- Intermittent player/NPC movement faults remain under investigation, including Music Lab returns and Hypno Pan/Act 3: Montage. Leaving and returning helped one player-movement reproduction.
- Windowed presentation stutters, occasional song/Combo Bucket hitches and repeated exclusive-fullscreen failures at launch remain unresolved. Fullscreen helped one song reproduction but is not a universal fix.
- An AP cassette receipt (Zen) once became available only after restart. The delayed-delivery case remains open.
- Already-owned Hypno Pan can prevent its native crafting interaction; ability use works.
- Tower exterior routing is build/regression-tested, not gameplay-tested. Fresh chest paths and receipt-order combinations still need broader acceptance.
- Demonic Tower, Demonic Escape, Demonic Lockers and Demolition Certificate were not individually replayed in the broad pass. Local co-op and cross-player/multiworld coverage remain incomplete.

See [known issues](https://github.com/Akamarus/Super-Crazy-Rhythm-Castle-Archipelago/blob/main/docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md).
The project remains experimental despite the verified end-to-end victory.
