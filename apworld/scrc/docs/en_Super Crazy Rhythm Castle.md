# Super Crazy Rhythm Castle

Start at the Phone Hub with Roots Access. The other five castle areas each have an access item in the multiworld. The Music Lab and Game Garage are available from the hub. A location check sends the item placed there by the seed, which can belong to any player.

## Stars and victory

The multiworld contains 66 AP Stars for your slot. Each campaign entrance has a seed-specific Star requirement. Star Eaters count the Stars you own and leave them in your inventory.

The default goal is King Ferdinand. Reach your configured Star goal and then clear Level 22. Bunker progression is enabled by default and adds five randomized items and six checks. With Bunker progression enabled, you can choose True Ending as the goal. That route requires the Bunker Keycard and Game Ender. A Game Ender received from the multiworld can be used before you finish the crafting quest.

## Music Lab and Game Garage

The 30 cassettes can be individual items or shuffled into pairs or triples. Deposit the songs you receive to play them in the Music Lab. Five cassette sources share the existing 32-, 64-, 89-, 111- and 140-point chests.

Music Lab Point items supply 180 points across the seed. Nine chests open at 5, 10, 20, 32, 46, 64, 89, 111 and 140 points. The pool can combine point denominations when excluded locations leave too few slots for progression.

Five Game Garage cartridges are randomized. Collect Vampire Killer at its native entrance pickup. Your AP difficulty determines which medal checks each song offers.

## AP performance difficulty

The in-game Regular/Pro choice is independent of AP difficulty. Each row includes the tiers from the previous row.

| AP difficulty | Campaign checks added | Song medal added | Bunker disabled | Bunker enabled |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Normal | Completion and one Star | Bronze | 164 | 170 |
| Hard | Two Stars | Silver | 222 | 228 |
| Expert | Three Stars | Gold | 280 | 286 |
| Perfection | Same as Expert | Platinum | 316 | 322 |

## Quest pickups

In Roots, Weed Killer lets you enter Level 3 and reach Frog and Hippo's Plant Pipes pickup. If you reach them before receiving Plant Pipes, collect the check, exit through the normal menu, and return with Plant Pipes to complete the level.

The Old Game Data and Car Battery hand-ins each require their matching item. Their character rewards can arrive separately through the multiworld.
